CashFlow – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

CashFlow ("the app") is a personal expense tracker for Android, published by Studio Dub ("we"). This policy explains how the app handles your information. In short: everything you enter stays on your phone. We never receive it, and we do not sell or share it.

Information the app stores on your phone

The app saves the information you enter: expenses (amounts, descriptions, categories, dates), budgets, recurring expenses, your theme and currency settings, the date of your last backup, and compressed copies of any receipt photos you attach. All of it is kept only in the app's private storage on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

No accounts, no internet, no tracking

  • The app has no accounts or sign-in.
  • The app does not have internet access and does not connect to any server.
  • The app requests no Android permissions that give access to your personal data, such as camera, location, contacts or storage.
  • The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services and no third-party tracking code.
  • The app shows its screens with Android System WebView and opts out of WebView's diagnostic data upload.
  • Android's automatic backup is turned off for this app, so your data is not copied to your Google account or moved to a new phone automatically. Use Export backup and Import backup to move it yourself.

Separately from the app, Google Play and Android may collect information about your device and app usage (for example, crash reports if you have allowed this in your phone's settings) under Google's Privacy Policy.

Photos and files

When you attach a receipt photo or import a backup, Android's file picker lets you choose the file. The app only reads the file you select. For receipts, it saves a compressed copy in its own storage; the original photo is not changed. A backup file is read once to import its contents and is not kept. The app cannot see any other photos or files.

Backups you create

When you use Export backup, the app creates a file containing all your expenses, budgets, recurring expenses and receipt photos, and Android lets you choose where to save it (for example your phone's Downloads folder or Google Drive). While saving, the app briefly keeps a temporary copy in its private storage and deletes it afterwards. The backup file is not encrypted or password-protected: anyone who can open it can read its contents. Where you store or share it is under your control and subject to the policies of the storage service you choose.

How your data is protected

Android keeps each app's private storage separate, so other apps cannot read CashFlow's data. The app does not add its own encryption, so we recommend protecting your phone with a screen lock and keeping exported backup files somewhere safe.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. We do not hold any copy. You can delete expenses (together with their receipt photos), budgets and recurring expenses in the app at any time. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, permanently deletes everything the app has stored on your phone. Backup files you exported are not affected; you can delete them yourself.

Children

The app is not directed at children. Because it does not collect information from anyone, it does not collect information from children.
